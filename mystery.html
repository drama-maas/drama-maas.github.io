Mystery Dinner

Turmoil at the Talent Show

Someone is trying to shut down the Mystic Falls Talent Show, and every contestant is a suspect. Pick who you want to be.

The story

The Mystic Falls Talent Show is days away, and contestants are arriving from all over for a shot at the grand prize: a spot on World Talent Search.

But the contest organizer, Dr. Black, has a problem. Someone keeps trying to wreck the show. Posters have gone missing, the show's website and social media were hacked, and the local paper even reported that the contest was cancelled.

You are one of the contestants. You've practiced your act all year, and you really want the saboteur caught. The trouble is, the saboteur is one of you.

Choose your character

  2. Pick your first, second and third choices.

How the night works

  • Mingle and snoopYou get a card with things to share and secrets to keep. Talk to everyone and figure out who's hiding what.
  • Talent show, Act OneHalf the contestants perform a short act, about one to two minutes.
  • The plot thickensThe saboteur strikes again. New clues come out and the questioning gets serious.
  • Accuse and revealEveryone makes their guess, then each character tells their side until the saboteur confesses.
  • Talent show, Act TwoThe rest of the contestants perform to close out the night.

You don't need a real talent. Performing is optional, and you can do any act you like. Each character's act from last year is listed below only as an idea. Stay in character all night, and have fun with your character's odd habits!
