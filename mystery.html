Mystery Dinner

Turmoil at the Talent Show

Someone is trying to shut down the Mystic Falls Talent Show, and every contestant is a suspect. Your table gets dinner, the run of the questioning, and one chance to name the saboteur.

Friday 23 October · doors open at 6:00pm

OCT 23 Get tickets OCT 23

The story

The Mystic Falls Talent Show is days away, and contestants are arriving from all over for a shot at the grand prize: a spot on World Talent Search.

But the contest organizer, Dr. Black, has a problem. Someone keeps trying to wreck the show. Posters have gone missing, the show's website and social media were hacked, and the local paper even reported that the contest was cancelled.

Every contestant swears it wasn't them. One of them is lying, and by the end of the night it is your job to work out which one.

How the night works

  1. Find your tableEvery table has its own full cast of suspects, so the mystery plays out around you rather than on a stage. Dinner is served while it does.
  2. Meet the suspectsThe contestants introduce themselves and mingle. Ask them anything. Every one of them is hiding something, and every one has a habit or two they cannot help — spotting those is half the game.
  3. Talent show, Act OneHalf the contestants perform their acts.
  4. The plot thickensThe saboteur strikes again. Fresh clues come out and the questioning gets serious.
  5. Make your accusationEveryone names the culprit they think did it, and then each character tells their side until the saboteur confesses.
  6. Talent show, Act TwoThe rest of the contestants perform to close out the night.

The suspects stay in character all evening and will not break for anything. Question them, compare notes with your table, and see who gets it right.
